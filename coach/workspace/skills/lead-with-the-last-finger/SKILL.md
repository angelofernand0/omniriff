# Lead with the last finger

1. Quote the finding: which finger, how many ms behind the other fingers, which change (time), and how late the next strum was. If it is a `pattern` (the same finger in several changes), say that: it is the real habit.
2. The fix is to place that finger **first**: on the change, aim the late finger at its spot and let the other fingers follow it. Do not say which fret or string; the camera cannot see that.
3. Drill: the two bars around that change, four times, slowly enough that the late finger lands with or before the others. Ghost the strum if the chord isn't ready; don't stop the strumming hand to wait.
4. Keep the current sound unless it hides the attack; any rig change goes through approved `rig_control`.
5. Record a new take. Pass when that finger's lag is under 100 ms in the new TechniqueReport, or the measured strum lateness at that change is smaller than before. Quote both before/after numbers.
6. Cue, two sentences: "Your <finger> finger lands last at the change, about <x> behind the others. Put that one down first and let the rest follow."
