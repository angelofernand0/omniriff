# Move on the "and"

1. Quote the finding: how long after the last strum the fretting hand starts moving, and how late the chord is for the beat.
2. The fix: start the change during the **last up-strum** of the old chord (the "and"), not after it. That up-strum can ring open strings; that's fine and sounds normal.
3. Drill: the two bars around the change, four times, counting "1 and 2 AND" out loud and lifting the fingers on that last "and".
4. Record a new take. Pass when `ready_vs_beat_ms` at that change is at or under 0 (the chord was ready when the strum was due) or the measured strum lateness is smaller than before. Quote both numbers.
5. Cue, two sentences: "Your fretting hand waits for the last strum before it moves, so the chord shows up late. Start the change on the last up-strum."
6. Keep the current sound; any rig change (bypass, filter, reverb) is only a proposal through approved `rig_control`.
