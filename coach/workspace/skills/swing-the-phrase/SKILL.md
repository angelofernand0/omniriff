# Swing the phrase

1. Quote the finding: which phrase (its number and time), its swing ratio against the rest of the take (say "straight" vs "swung", e.g. "even, where the rest is long-short"), and/or its attack spread in dB against the rest ("your attacks jump around there").
2. The fix, in one breath: swing that phrase like the others, long-short, and hit every strum the same. Picture the long one on the beat and the short one leading into the next.
3. Drill: loop just that phrase four times. Count "long-short, long-short" out loud and keep the pick the same depth on every strum; let the strumming arm swing the whole time.
4. Taking the effects off makes the attacks easier to hear; propose it through approved `rig_control` (bypass), never change it yourself.
5. Record a new take. Pass when that phrase's swing ratio is within about 0.2 of the rest of the take and its attack spread is no longer flagged. Quote both before/after numbers.
6. Cue, two sentences: "It's the third phrase: you're playing it straight and your attacks jump around. Swing it, long-short, and hit every strum the same."
