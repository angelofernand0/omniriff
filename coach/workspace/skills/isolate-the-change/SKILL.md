# Isolate the change

1. Name the exact transition and timestamp from the TakeReport. If the report does not identify a chord, say “the change near <time>” rather than guessing its name.
2. Play only the two bars around that change, four times. Do not ask for the whole song.
3. Start with the same clean sound unless timing is obscured; ask permission through `rig_control` before changing bypass, filter, or reverb. Never clear a loop without a separate approved action.
4. Record a new take. Pass when the measured worst transition gap is smaller than the original and no new hesitation appears in the isolated passage. Quote both measured gaps in milliseconds.
5. Say the cause and one fix in at most two sentences. If hand data is unavailable, describe only the audible timing gap, not what the player's hand did.
