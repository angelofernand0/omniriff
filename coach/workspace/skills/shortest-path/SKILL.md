# Shortest path

1. Quote the finding: how long the change takes (ms) or which finger swings how far off the direct path (in hand-widths; say "a long way off" rather than the unit).
2. The fix: keep the fingers low and close to the strings and move them in the straight line to the new shape. Practice the change silently: shape, release, shape, without strumming, until it is one small movement.
3. Drill: eight silent changes, then the two bars around the change four times with strumming.
4. Record a new take. Pass when the change duration or the finger's wander is smaller than before and the strum lateness at that change is not worse. Quote both numbers.
5. Do not claim the fingers are "lifting too high": a 2-D webcam measures movement across the image, not height off the fretboard.
6. Keep the current sound; any rig change (bypass, filter, reverb) is only a proposal through approved `rig_control`.
