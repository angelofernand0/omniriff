# Dry check

1. Point to the measured hesitation or uneven interval in the TakeReport. Effects masking it is a hypothesis, not a measured fact.
2. Propose `rig_control(control="bypass")` and wait for the player's approval. If declined, leave the rig as it is and continue with a timing-only drill.
3. Play the two bars around the finding four times with the dry sound, then record a take. The player can restore the original sound manually; an agent restore also requires approval.
4. Pass when the new TakeReport shows a smaller worst gap or fewer hesitations in that passage. Quote before and after values in milliseconds.
5. Say one cause supported by the reports and one practical fix, in at most two sentences.
