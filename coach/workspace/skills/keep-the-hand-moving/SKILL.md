# Keep the hand moving

1. Confirm a strumming-hand stall overlaps the timing gap within the take. Quote both timestamps and durations. If there is no HandReport, choose a timing-only skill instead; do not infer a freeze from audio.
2. Rehearse the two bars around that change four times. Keep the strumming arm swinging like a pendulum through the change, even if the fretting hand is late. Quiet ghost strums are allowed.
3. Keep the current sound unless it hides the attack; any bypass or other rig change goes through approved `rig_control`.
4. Record another take. Pass when the aligned hand stall is absent or shorter, and the worst audible gap is smaller than before. Quote both reports' measured before/after values; if camera data are missing, judge only the audio gap.
5. Say the observed cause and the pendulum fix in at most two sentences, without blaming the player.
