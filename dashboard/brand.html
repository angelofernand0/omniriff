The mark is Riff, the coach, drawn on a 16-cell grid: a ridged knob with eyes. Keep one mark-width of clear space around the lockup. Never stretch it, round it, smooth it, recolour the eyes or set the wordmark in a font.

01 COLOUR

Pink is the only brand colour and it means one thing: alive. The loop is playing, the hand has the control, the coach is awake. Red, amber and green are signals with one job each. Nothing else gets a colour.

05 ICONS

Six icons, eight by eight cells, one colour, drawn in brand.js. Shown at x8 and x2. One icon per module or input; no icon without a label beside it.

06 RIFF, THE COACH

Riff is the only round thing on the screen and the only thing that moves smoothly. The machine is square and moves in steps; the teacher is alive. Riff has eyes and no mouth: what it says is printed beside it. It turns like a knob when it changes a control, and it only moves when the coach is doing something. These are still portraits.
